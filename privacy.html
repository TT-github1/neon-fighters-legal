Privacy Policy — Neon Fighters

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Neon Fighters (霓虹战机) is a mobile game (bundle ID: com.xxdff.neonfighters) published by 何天夫. Contact: 2837852077@qq.com.

This page is the privacy policy for the App Store listing and for Google AdMob. The game Settings screen opens this same page.

What the game stores on your device

We do not create user accounts. We do not run a game server that keeps your progress. There is no score and no high score.

On your device the game stores:

  • Settings: music, sound, vibration, touch buttons, backdrop, language.
  • Gold in your account, hangar upgrades, and which crafts you unlocked.
  • Thunder trial counts if you watched a rewarded ad to fly Thunder.

Uninstalling the game deletes this local data. We cannot restore it from our side.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send. We use that only to answer you.

Advertising

The iOS version uses Google AdMob. Where ads are enabled, Google and its partners may collect device identifiers and advertising data to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads if you consent.

The current iOS version may show:

  • An interstitial ad after a sortie ends.
  • Optional rewarded ads: double that sortie’s gold, start Thunder trials, or continue after lives are spent.

You can skip the gold-double ad. Refusing tracking does not block the game. Rewarded ads may still play.

See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Europe, UK and Switzerland

If you are in these regions, the game shows a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent message before personalized ads. You can refuse. You can also change tracking permission in the system Settings app.

United States state privacy

In some US states, sharing device identifiers with advertising partners can count as a “sale” or “share” of personal information. If the law requires it, the game shows a Google UMP message so you can allow or refuse that sharing.

iOS tracking

On iOS, the system may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission so ads can be more relevant. You can refuse. Refusing does not block the game. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13. We do not tag ad requests as child-directed.

What we do not collect ourselves

We do not collect your name, payment card, precise location, photos, contacts, or microphone. We do not sell gold for real money in the current version, so we do not receive payment details. Apple or Google may still process payments for the store itself.

Contact

For privacy questions, email 2837852077@qq.com.

隐私协议 — 霓虹战机

最近更新：2026 年 9 月 5 日

霓虹战机（Neon Fighters）是一款手机游戏（包名 com.xxdff.neonfighters），发布者何天夫。 联系：2837852077@qq.com。

本页用于 App Store 与 Google AdMob。游戏设置里的隐私协议会打开同一页。

设备上留下什么

我们不开用户账号。没有把进度存在我们自己的服务器上。没有分数，也没有最高分。

游戏只写在你这台设备上：

  • 设置：音乐、音效、震动、触控按钮、背景、语言。
  • 账户里的金币、机库升级、已解锁的战机。
  • 若你看激励开雷霆，会记下试用次数。

卸掉游戏，这些本地数据一起删掉。我们这边无法帮你找回。

你发邮件过来时，我们会收到你的邮箱和正文。只用来回信。

广告

iOS 版使用 Google AdMob。在出广告的平台上，Google 及其合作方可能收集设备标识和广告数据，用来投放和衡量广告。你同意后才出个性化广告。

当前 iOS 版可能出现：

  • 出击结束后的插屏。
  • 可选的激励：把这次金币再写入一笔、开雷霆试用、残机用尽后再进场。

这次金币翻倍可以跳过。拒绝追踪不会挡住游戏。激励仍可能播放。

Google 政策： https://policies.google.com/privacy 与 https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads。

欧洲、英国、瑞士

在这些地区，个性化广告前会出 Google UMP 同意表。可以拒绝。 也可以在系统设置里改追踪权限。

美国部分州

在部分美国州，把设备标识交给广告合作方可能算「出售」或「共享」。法律要求时，游戏会出 Google UMP 表，让你允许或拒绝。

iOS 追踪

iOS 可能询问 App Tracking Transparency，用来让广告更贴你。可以拒绝。拒绝不会挡住游戏。 之后可在 iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 里改。

儿童

本游戏不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象。广告请求不会标成面向儿童。

我们自己不收集什么

我们不收集姓名、银行卡、精确位置、照片、通讯录、麦克风。当前版本不卖金币，我们收不到支付信息。商店本身的付款仍由苹果或 Google 处理。

联系

隐私问题请发 2837852077@qq.com。
